# Collections quickstart for invited teachers

Original Collections (in the app: Collections) is a locked, in-browser writing room, a digital replacement for the blue book exam. You set a question, your
students write in it for a set time, and you read, mark and return their work
in one place. Your workspace is private to you and uses Collections only: it does
not build writing profiles or score anyone's writing.

## 1. Set your password

1. Open the email **"Your Original Collections workspace is ready"** and follow the link.
   It works once and expires after 14 days; if it has expired, ask support for
   a new one.
2. On **Set your password**, type a **New password** (at least 8 characters)
   and the same again in **Confirm password**.
3. Tick **I agree to the terms of service and privacy policy.** (the links
   open the documents), then press **Save and continue**.

You land in your own empty workspace: bookmark that page. Next time, open it,
press **Sign in**, enter your **Email** and **Password**, and press **Enter**.

## 2. Create a course and invite students

1. Open **Courses** in the top bar and press **+ New course**. Give it a
   **Course name** (the **Code** and **Term** are optional) and press
   **Create course**. The course's roster opens.
2. Under **Add students**, paste your students' email addresses, one per line
   (`Name, email` also works). Keep **Email each student their invitation**
   ticked and press **Add students**.
3. Each student gets an email ("You've been added to Original Collections for" your
   course) with a one-time link to set their password. If email is not
   working, use **Copy all links** or **Download links (CSV)** and send the
   links yourself. **Fresh links for all *N* waiting** replaces links that
   were lost or have expired.

Two other ways onto the same roster:

- **Upload class list:** press **Upload class list** and choose a CSV, either
  your Canvas gradebook export (**Grades** → **Export**) or any file with name
  and email columns. Check the matched columns, then the preview: new
  students, students already on the roster, and rows with problems. A Canvas
  export usually has no email column; enter your school's email domain to make
  addresses from each student's login (`jdoe` becomes `jdoe@school.edu`), or
  skip those students. **Invite *N* new students** sends the invitations as
  above. The file stays on your computer and is not kept.
- **Join link:** under **Join link**, tick **Let students join this course
  with a link or code** and share the link or the six-character code (students
  can type it on the sign-in page). Each student enters their name and email
  and chooses a password. You can limit joining to one email domain, and tick
  **Approve each student before they join**: they then show as **Pending** on
  the roster until you press **Approve** (or **Decline**). **Reset code** stops
  the old link and code at once. Untick the box to turn joining off.

## 3. Set a writing session

There are two ways:

- **Quick session:** open **Collections** in the top bar. Under **Start a new
  session**, write the question (**+ Add a second question** if you want
  more), choose the class under **Which class?**, set **How long do they
  get?**, and press **Open the booklets**. It is open to that class straight
  away; **Manage session and read submissions →** takes you to it.
- **Full examination:** open **Examinations** and press **+ New examination**.
  Fill in the **Examination Title**, **Course**, **Duration** (minutes) and the
  questions. **Opens**, **Closes** and the word limits are optional. Under
  **Secure Lockdown** you can choose **Full-screen, tabs watched** and
  **Block copy & paste**. Press **Publish Examination** to make it visible to
  the course, or **Save as Draft** to finish it later.

To change it later, open **Examinations** and click its title. In
**Settings**, the **Status** can be **Draft (hidden from students)**,
**Published** or **Closed**; press **Save changes**. **Preview** shows it as a
student would see it, and **Watch live** shows who is writing and who has
submitted.

## 4. What your students see

1. They set a password from their invitation link (as you did, without the
   terms box), sign in and see **My exams**. An open session has an **Open**
   button.
2. A briefing page shows the title, the time allowed and the conditions.
   Pressing **Begin Examination** starts the timer, which cannot be paused.
3. They write in the booklet (**← Previous** and **Next →** move between
   questions). Their writing is saved on their device as they go. If the page
   reloads or the browser closes, they open the exam again from **My exams**
   on the same device (the button now says **Resume**): their writing comes
   back, and the timer has kept running.
4. When they are done they press **Seal & Submit** and confirm "Seal and
   submit now? You cannot change your answers after sealing." If you set a
   minimum word count, the button waits until they reach it. When time runs
   out, whatever is written is sealed for them.
5. A receipt, **Examination Sealed**, shows the title, course, their name and
   word count, and **✓ Delivered to your teacher** once their work has reached
   the server. If it did not arrive they see **We could not deliver your
   examination** and a **Try Again** button; their writing stays on the device.

## 5. Read, mark and return work

1. Open the session (**Examinations** → its title, or **Overview** →
   **Mark and release →**). Every sealed submission is listed under
   **Submissions**; the **Submissions** tab in the top bar lists all of them.
2. Press **Read & mark** to read one. Enter a **Mark (optional)** and
   **Feedback for the student (optional)**, then **Save mark and feedback**.
   Students do not see anything yet.
3. When you are ready, press **Release results** on the session's page.
   Students then see their mark and feedback under **My submissions** →
   **Read →**. **Hide results** takes them away again.
4. **Export CSV** on the session's page downloads one row per submission:
   name, email, when it was submitted, word count, minutes taken, whether it
   was late, number of warnings, mark, feedback and the full text.

**Lockdown warnings are context, not evidence.** The reader lists events such
as "Left full-screen" or "Hid the tab". A student may do these by accident;
talk to them before drawing any conclusion.

## 6. Passwords and deleting data

- **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page asks for your **Email** and
  sends a reset link (**Email me a reset link**). The link opens **Set your
  password** again. While signed in, you can change it under **Account** →
  **Change password**.
- A student who lost their link: open the course roster and use **New invite
  link**, or **Password reset link** if they have already signed in.
- **Remove** takes a student off one course and keeps their work.
  **Delete student** (on **Students** or the course roster) permanently
  erases the student, every exam they sat and their place on every course.
  It cannot be undone.
- For anything else, such as deleting your whole workspace, contact
  [Andrew: support contact].
