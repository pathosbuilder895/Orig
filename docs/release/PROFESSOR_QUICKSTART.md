# Collections quickstart for invited teachers

Original Collections (in the app: Collections) is a locked, in-browser writing room, a digital replacement for the blue book exam. You set a question, your
students write in it for a set time, and you read, mark and return their work
in one place. Each piece of writing you set is a **collection**. Your
workspace is private to you and uses Collections only: it does not build
writing profiles or score anyone's writing.

## 1. Set your password

1. Open the email **"Your Original Collections workspace is ready"** and follow the link.
   It works once and expires after 14 days; if it has expired, ask support for
   a new one.
2. On **Set your password**, type a **New password** (at least 8 characters)
   and the same again in **Confirm password**.
3. Tick **I agree to the terms of service and privacy policy.** (the links
   open the documents), then press **Save and continue**.

You land on your **Overview**: bookmark that page. Next time, open it, press
**Sign in**, enter your **Email** and **Password**, and press **Enter**.

The bar along the top has **Overview**, **Collections**, **Courses**,
**Students**, **Submissions** and **Proctor**.

## 2. Create a course and add students

1. On the **Overview**, press **Create your first course →** (later, use
   **Courses** → **+ New course**). Give it a **Course name** (the **Code**
   and **Term** are optional) and press **Create course**. The course's
   roster opens.
2. Under **Add students**, paste your students' email addresses, one per line
   (`Name, email` also works), and press **Add students**. If email is set up
   for your workspace, tick **Email each student their invitation**; if it is
   not, that box explains why it is greyed out.
3. Each student gets a one-time link to set their password. The links are
   shown only once, so use **Copy all invite links** or **Download links
   (CSV)** and send them yourself if they were not emailed. **Fresh links for
   all *N* waiting** replaces links that were lost or have expired. A student
   on two of your courses can use either course's link; the first one they
   use is the one that counts.
4. **Or share one link for the whole course.** Under **Sign-up link**, press
   **Turn on sign-up link** and copy it. Anyone who opens it makes their own
   student account and joins this course. **Replace link** stops the old one
   working; **Turn off** stops it altogether.

## 3. Set a collection

1. Open **Collections** and press **+ New collection**.
2. Fill in **I. Identity** (the **Collection Title** and the **Course**),
   **II. Time & Length** (the **Duration**, and optional **Minimum Words** and
   **Maximum Words**), and **III. Prompts** (**+ Add another question** for
   more than one, up to 20).
3. **More options** holds the rest: in class or take-home, timed or not, an
   **Opens** and **Closes** window, **Secure Lockdown** (**Full-screen, tabs
   watched**, **Block copy & paste**, **No AI assistants**) and **Phone
   parking**. **No AI assistants** is an instruction to students: a web page
   cannot block other apps, and the briefing says so.
4. Press **Publish Collection** to make it visible to the course, or **Save
   as Draft** to finish it later.

To change it later, open **Collections** and click its title. In
**Settings**, the **Status** can be **Draft (hidden from students)**,
**Published** or **Closed**; press **Save changes**. **Preview** shows it as a
student would see it, and **Watch live** shows who is writing and who has
submitted.

## 4. What your students see

1. They set a password from their link (ticking that they have read the
   student notice), sign in and see **My collections**. An open collection has
   an **Open** button.
2. A briefing page shows the title, the time allowed and **How this
   collection works**: exactly which conditions are on. Pressing **Begin
   writing** starts the timer, which cannot be paused.
3. They write in the booklet (**← Previous** and **Next →** move between
   questions). Their writing is saved as they go, on the device and on the
   server. If the page reloads or the browser closes, they open the
   collection again from **My collections**, on any browser (the button now
   says **Resume**): their writing comes back, and the timer has kept running.
4. When they are done they press **Seal & Submit**, and the booklet asks
   **Ready to submit?** with **Keep writing** and **Seal and submit**. If you
   set a minimum word count, the button waits until they reach it. When time
   runs out, whatever is written is sealed for them.
5. A receipt, **Collection Sealed**, shows the title, course, their name and
   word count, and **✓ Delivered to your teacher** once their work has reached
   the server. If it did not arrive they are told so, with a **Try again**
   button; their writing stays on the device.

## 5. Read, mark and return work

1. Open the collection (**Collections** → its title, or **Overview** →
   **Mark and release →**). Every sealed booklet is listed under
   **Submissions**; the **Submissions** tab in the top bar lists all of them.
2. Press **Read & mark** to read one. Enter a **Mark (optional)** and
   **Feedback for the student (optional)**, then **Save mark and feedback**.
   Students do not see anything yet. **Print** and **Download as text** keep a
   copy.
3. When you are ready, press **Release results** on the collection's page.
   Students then see their mark and feedback under **My submissions** →
   **Read →**. **Hide results** takes them away again.
4. **Export CSV** on the collection's page downloads one row per submission:
   name, email, when it was submitted, word count, minutes taken, whether it
   was late, number of warnings, mark, feedback and the full text.

**Lockdown notes are context, not evidence.** The reader lists events such
as "Left full-screen" or "Hid the tab". A student may do these by accident;
talk to them before drawing any conclusion.

## 6. Passwords and deleting data

- **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page asks for your **Email** and
  sends a reset link (**Email me a reset link**). A reset link works once and
  lasts 24 hours. While signed in, you can change your password under
  **Account** → **Change password**. Either way, every other place you were
  signed in is signed out.
- A student who lost their link: open the course roster and use **New invite
  link**, or **Password reset link** if they have already set a password.
- **Remove** takes a student off one course and keeps their work.
  **Delete student** (on **Students** or the course roster) permanently
  erases the student, every collection they sat and their place on every
  course. It cannot be undone.
- For anything else, such as deleting your whole workspace, contact
  [Andrew: support contact].
