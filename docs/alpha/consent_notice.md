# Alpha test participation notice (for Andrew's review; procedure updated 2026-10-30)

**What this is.** You're invited to help test a writing app, Original, before it's used more widely. This alpha is a **calibration** exercise: we're checking whether its writing-consistency feature, which is still in testing, compares new writing with a person's earlier writing in a sensible way, and whether the site handles people writing at different times. You won't be graded. Nothing here is coursework, and **no conclusions will be drawn about you or anyone else.** The test checks the software, not the people.

**Who can take part.** Adults aged 18 or older. Taking part is completely voluntary. The alpha starts on 2026-10-19 and is open-ended: you do your parts when you can, and Andrew will tell you when he closes your participation or the alpha as a whole.

**What you'll do.**
1. Share **at least three of your own past papers** (300+ words in total) that you wrote yourself. You can upload them yourself in the app (**Share a past paper** on your Collections home page: a PDF, Word or text file, or pasted text), or email them to Andrew at arclark555@gmail.com and he uploads them. Either way, Andrew reviews each paper before it is used as your reference writing, and you see only whether it was accepted, never any reading of it. **If you don't have three separate papers, one long paper may, with your permission, be split into sections used as reference writing** (in the app, tick "This is a section of a longer paper" and name the longer paper). Those pieces are labelled "split-derived" in the test data, are kept separate from genuinely separate papers in the results, and are never quietly mixed in with them.
2. Sign in with a personal link and open the alpha exam in Collections. It has two questions:
   - **Question 1:** paste a different piece of your own writing, one you wrote yourself that is not one of your three past papers.
   - **Question 2:** ask an AI tool of your choice (for example ChatGPT or Claude) to write about the same topic as your question 1 piece, and paste what it wrote. **Give the AI tool the topic only, never your own writing or anyone else's.**
   Then choose **Seal & Submit**. The app's **Instructions** button repeats these steps whenever you need them.
3. Fill in a short feedback form, which also asks the name of the AI tool you used.

**Your past papers and the two pieces.** The writing-consistency feature analyzes your past papers and both pieces in the alpha exam, measuring things like sentence length, word choice and punctuation habits, and compares them. The question 2 piece is there to check that the software notices writing that is not yours. **The AI tool is a separate service with its own terms.** You use it yourself, outside this app, and Original never sends anything to it. With your permission, Andrew may also use **one of your papers as a shadow test sample** attributed to another participant, and staff may create a shadow sample in your voice using a writing tool, as described in the form questions. Each of these needs your separate consent below, and you can say no to them and still take part. Please remove anything private, such as names, grades or health details, from the papers and the question 1 piece before you upload or paste them.

**What's collected.**
- Your name and email (for signing in) and your password (stored only in scrambled form).
- Your uploaded past papers, the two pieces you paste into the alpha exam, the name of the AI tool you used, the measurements calculated from them, and the consistency results.
- When you start and submit, and basic session events (such as switching tabs, if that setting is on).
- **Request logs record your IP address and browser type** each time your browser contacts the site, as standard web logs do.
- Your feedback answers.

**Who sees it.** Andrew Clark and the people who run the app, only to run and check the test. **You won't see any scores, and neither will other participants.** Nothing is sold or used for advertising. The site runs on Render.

**Stopping.** You can stop at any time, for any reason, with no consequences, and ask for your data to be deleted early.

**Deletion.** **Your data will be deleted within 14 days after you withdraw or Andrew closes your participation, whichever comes first. Encrypted backups age out within about 30 days after that deletion.** That covers your account, uploaded papers, the pieces you pasted, measurements and results, along with Andrew's notes linking papers to people.

**Questions or deletion requests:** Andrew Clark, arclark555@gmail.com
