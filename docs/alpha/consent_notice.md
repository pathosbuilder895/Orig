# Alpha test participation notice (DRAFT for Andrew's review)

**What this is.** You're invited to help test a writing app, Original, before it's used more widely. We're testing two things: whether the site handles about 20 people writing at once, and whether its **writing-consistency feature, which is still in testing**, works properly on real writing. You won't be graded. Nothing here is coursework, and **no conclusions will be drawn about you or anyone else.** The test checks the software, not the people.

**Who can take part.** Adults aged 18 or older. Taking part is completely voluntary.

**What you'll do.**
1. Send Andrew **at least three of your own past papers** (300+ words in total) that you wrote yourself. He uploads them as your reference writing.
2. Sign in with a personal link and write two short timed papers (about 25 minutes each) on made-up prompts. One is in your own voice. In the other, you **imitate the style of a partner** who has agreed to it.
3. Fill in a short feedback form.

**Your past papers and your partner.** The writing-consistency feature analyzes your past papers and both of your new papers, measuring things like sentence length, word choice and punctuation habits, and compares them. To make the imitation exercise possible, **your partner will read one or two of your uploaded papers**, and you'll read one or two of theirs. With your permission, Andrew may also use **one of your papers as a "not-own" test sample** attributed to another participant, to check that the software notices it. Each of these needs your separate consent below, and you can say no to them and still take part. Please remove anything private, such as names, grades or health details, from the papers before you send them.

**What's collected.**
- Your name and email (for signing in) and your password (stored only in scrambled form).
- Your uploaded past papers, the two papers you write, the measurements calculated from them, and the consistency results.
- When you start and submit, and basic session events (such as switching tabs, if that setting is on).
- **Request logs record your IP address and browser type** each time your browser contacts the site, as standard web logs do.
- Your feedback answers.

**Who sees it.** Andrew Clark and the people who run the app, only to run and check the test. **You won't see any scores, and neither will other participants.** Your partner sees only the excerpts you agreed to share. Nothing is sold or used for advertising. The site runs on Render.

**Stopping.** You can stop at any time, for any reason, with no consequences, and ask for your data to be deleted early.

**Deletion.** All accounts, uploaded papers, written papers, measurements and results are deleted within **14 days** after the test, along with Andrew's notes linking papers to people. Encrypted backups age out within about **30 days**.
