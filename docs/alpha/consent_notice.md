# Alpha test participation notice (for Andrew's review; procedure updated 2026-11, four papers)

**What this is.** You're invited to help test a writing app, Original, before it's used more widely. This alpha is a **calibration** exercise: we're checking whether its writing-consistency feature, which is still in testing, compares new writing with a person's earlier writing in a sensible way, and whether the site handles people writing at different times. You won't be graded. Nothing here is coursework, and **no conclusions will be drawn about you or anyone else.** The test checks the software, not the people.

**Who can take part.** Adults aged 18 or older. Taking part is completely voluntary. The alpha starts on 2026-10-19 and is open-ended: you do your parts when you can, and Andrew will tell you when he closes your participation or the alpha as a whole.

**What you'll do.**
1. Share **four of your own past papers** that you wrote yourself. You can upload them yourself in the app (**Share a past paper** on your Collections home page: a PDF, Word or text file, or pasted text), or email them to Andrew at arclark555@gmail.com and he uploads them. Andrew reviews each paper, and you see only whether it is waiting, accepted or not used, never any reading of it. **If you don't have four separate papers, one long paper may, with your permission, be split into sections** (in the app, tick "This is a section of a longer paper" and name the longer paper). Those pieces are labelled "split-derived" in the test data, are kept separate from genuinely separate papers in the results, and are never quietly mixed in with them.
2. That's all. There is no exam to sit and nothing new to write. The app's **Instructions** button repeats these steps whenever you need them.
3. Afterwards, if you like, fill in a short feedback form about how the upload went.

**What happens with your papers.** Andrew keeps three of your papers as your reference writing and sets one aside, so one paper may keep showing as waiting for review. To check the software, he writes one extra piece imitating your style, with help from an AI writing tool, using your reference papers as the example. The writing-consistency feature then measures things like sentence length, word choice and punctuation habits, and compares your set-aside paper and his imitation with your three reference papers. **The AI writing tool is a separate service with its own terms.** Andrew uses it himself, outside this app; Original never sends anything to it, and you are never asked to use one. The imitation is labelled as his in the test data, is never presented as your work, and is not shown to other participants. With your separate permission, Andrew may also use **one of your papers as a shadow test sample** attributed to another participant; you can say no to that and still take part. Please remove anything private, such as names, grades or health details, from your papers before you share them.

**What's collected.**
- Your name and email (for signing in) and your password (stored only in scrambled form).
- Your uploaded past papers, the measurements calculated from them, Andrew's imitation of your style and its measurements, and the consistency results.
- When you sign in and share a paper.
- **Request logs record your IP address and browser type** each time your browser contacts the site, as standard web logs do.
- Your feedback answers.

**Who sees it.** Andrew Clark and the people who run the app, only to run and check the test. The AI writing tool Andrew uses for the imitation receives the reference papers he gives it as examples, under that tool's own terms. **You won't see any scores, and neither will other participants.** Nothing is sold or used for advertising. The site runs on Render.

**Stopping.** You can stop at any time, for any reason, with no consequences, and ask for your data to be deleted early.

**Deletion.** **Your data will be deleted within 14 days after you withdraw or Andrew closes your participation, whichever comes first. Encrypted backups age out within about 30 days after that deletion.** That covers your account, uploaded papers, Andrew's imitation of your style, measurements and results, along with Andrew's notes linking papers to people.

**Questions or deletion requests:** Andrew Clark, arclark555@gmail.com
