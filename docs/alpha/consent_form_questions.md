# Google Form "Original alpha test: participation and consent" (DRAFT)

Description: paste `consent_notice.md` in full.

1. **Full name** (short answer, required)
2. **Email you'll use for the test** (short answer, required, email validation)
3. **I am 18 years of age or older.** (checkbox, required)
4. **I have read the participation notice.** (checkbox, required)
5. **I understand that taking part is voluntary, I can stop at any time, and I can ask for early deletion.** (checkbox, required)
6. **I'll upload at least three past papers that I wrote myself, and I agree that they and my two new papers can be analyzed by a writing-consistency feature that is still in testing.** (checkbox, required)
7. **I understand this is not graded, I won't see any scores, and no conclusions will be drawn about me or anyone else.** (checkbox, required)
8. **I understand that what I write, session events, and request logs (IP address and browser type) are collected, and that everything, including my uploaded papers, is deleted within 14 days (backups age out within about 30 days).** (checkbox, required)
9. **I agree to write one paper imitating my assigned partner's style.** (checkbox, required)
10. **I agree that my assigned partner may read 1–2 of my uploaded papers so they can imitate my style.** (Yes / No, required. "No" means Andrew assigns you to a public-domain style instead.)
11. **I agree that one of my papers may be used as a "not-own" test sample attributed to another participant, so the software can be checked.** (Yes / No, required. Optional to agree.)
12. **I agree to take part in the alpha test.** (checkbox, required)
13. Accessibility needs or anything else we should know (paragraph, optional)

Settings: collect emails ON; responses go to a sheet only Andrew can see; delete it with the app data. Andrew pairs partners **only** among people who answered Yes to Q10, and uses papers as injected samples **only** from people who answered Yes to Q11.
