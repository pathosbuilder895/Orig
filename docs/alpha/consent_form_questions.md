# Google Form "Original alpha test: participation and consent"

Description: paste `consent_notice.md` in full. Build exactly from `google_forms_spec.md` §1.

1. **Full name** (short answer, required)
2. **Email you'll use for the test** (short answer, required, email validation)
3. **I am 18 years of age or older.** (checkbox, required)
4. **I have read the participation notice.** (checkbox, required)
5. **I understand that taking part is voluntary, I can stop at any time, and I can ask for early deletion.** (checkbox, required)
6. **I'll share four past papers that I wrote myself (in the app, or by email to Andrew as a fallback), and I agree that they can be analyzed by a writing-consistency feature that is still in testing.** (checkbox, required)
7. **Split-derived reference writing:** "If I don't have four separate past papers, I agree that one long paper I wrote may be split into sections (by me when uploading in the app, or by Andrew) and those sections used as my reference writing, labelled as split-derived in the test data." (Yes / No, required. "No" with fewer than 3 papers means that person is recorded as not having enough reference writing and is not scored.)
8. **I understand this is not graded, I won't see any scores, and no conclusions will be drawn about me or anyone else.** (checkbox, required)
9. **I understand that what I write, session events, and request logs (IP address and browser type) are collected, and that my data will be deleted within 14 days after I withdraw or Andrew closes my participation, whichever comes first (encrypted backups age out within about 30 days after that deletion).** (checkbox, required)
10. **I understand that Andrew will keep three of my papers as reference writing and set one aside, and that, to check the software, he will use an AI writing tool to write one piece imitating my style from my reference papers. It is labelled as his in the test data, never presented as my work, and not shown to other participants. I won't be asked to use an AI tool myself.** (checkbox, required. Four-paper design, 2026-11; needs Andrew's approval.)
11. **I agree that one of my papers may be used as a shadow test sample attributed to another participant, so the software can be checked.** (Yes / No, required. Optional to agree.)
12. *(Retired 2026-11, folded into question 10.)* On forms answered before then, Yes here is what allows Andrew's imitation for that volunteer; someone who answered No needs to agree to the new question 10 first.
13. **I agree to take part in the alpha test.** (checkbox, required)
14. Accessibility needs or anything else we should know (paragraph, optional)

Settings: collect emails ON; responses go to a sheet only Andrew can see; delete it per `deletion_plan.md`. Andrew uses papers as shadow samples **only** from people who answered Yes to Q11, and writes an imitation **only** for people who ticked the current Q10 (or, on a form answered before 2026-11, answered Yes to Q12). (Before 2026-10-30 this form had two partner-imitation questions; the alpha no longer pairs partners.)
