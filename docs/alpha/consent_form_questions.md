# Google Form "Original alpha test: participation and consent"

Description: paste `consent_notice.md` in full. Build exactly from `google_forms_spec.md` §1.

1. **Full name** (short answer, required)
2. **Email you'll use for the test** (short answer, required, email validation)
3. **I am 18 years of age or older.** (checkbox, required)
4. **I have read the participation notice.** (checkbox, required)
5. **I understand that taking part is voluntary, I can stop at any time, and I can ask for early deletion.** (checkbox, required)
6. **I'll upload at least three past papers that I wrote myself (in the app, or by email to Andrew as a fallback), and I agree that they and my two new papers can be analyzed by a writing-consistency feature that is still in testing.** (checkbox, required)
7. **Split-derived reference writing:** "If I don't have three separate past papers, I agree that one long paper I wrote may be split into sections (by me when uploading in the app, or by Andrew) and those sections used as my reference writing, labelled as split-derived in the test data." (Yes / No, required. "No" with fewer than 3 papers means that person is recorded as not having enough reference writing and is not scored.)
8. **I understand this is not graded, I won't see any scores, and no conclusions will be drawn about me or anyone else.** (checkbox, required)
9. **I understand that what I write, session events, and request logs (IP address and browser type) are collected, and that my data will be deleted within 14 days after I withdraw or Andrew closes my participation, whichever comes first (encrypted backups age out within about 30 days after that deletion).** (checkbox, required)
10. **I agree to write one paper imitating my assigned partner's style.** (checkbox, required)
11. **I agree that my assigned partner may read 1–2 of my uploaded papers so they can imitate my style.** (Yes / No, required. "No" means Andrew assigns you to a public-domain style instead.)
12. **I agree that one of my papers may be used as a shadow test sample attributed to another participant, so the software can be checked.** (Yes / No, required. Optional to agree.)
13. **I agree that staff (Andrew or someone working for him) may use a writing tool to create a shadow sample in my voice from one of my papers, labelled as staff-created, to check the software.** (Yes / No, required. Optional to agree; never created by me pasting my own work into a tool.)
14. **I agree to take part in the alpha test.** (checkbox, required)
15. Accessibility needs or anything else we should know (paragraph, optional)

Settings: collect emails ON; responses go to a sheet only Andrew can see; delete it per `deletion_plan.md`. Andrew pairs partners **only** among people who answered Yes to Q11, uses papers as shadow samples **only** from people who answered Yes to Q12, and creates staff tool-generated shadows **only** for people who answered Yes to Q13.
