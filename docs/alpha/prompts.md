# Prompts: two exams per volunteer (fictional, no coursework)

Both exams: 25 minutes, **Block copy & paste: ON**, "No AI assistants" ON, no word limits, spell check allowed. Writing consistency: OFF (see the runbook). Each exam allows one submission per student.

## Exam A: natural paper (write as yourself)
**Prompt 1: The Night Market.** A city has a market that opens only between midnight and dawn and sells things that can't be bought anywhere else. Describe one stall, its keeper and one customer. What is being bought, and what does it cost?

## Exam B: other-style paper (write as your partner)
Before the session, every volunteer is paired with a partner who has **agreed** to let them read 1–2 of their uploaded past papers and imitate their style. Andrew shares those excerpts privately. The pairs swap, and the person being imitated must have ticked the consent box.

**Prompt 2: The Letter from 2076, written in your partner's voice.** You receive a letter from a stranger living fifty years in the future who says they found your name in an old library. Write the reply **as your partner would write it**: their sentence length, their word choices, their habits. Don't copy their sentences.

## Spare prompt (if a third exam is needed or someone must redo one)
**Prompt 3: The Island Rulebook.** Twenty strangers are stranded on a small island with enough food for a month. Write the first five rules and argue for each one, then name one rule you decided against and why.

**Order on the day:** Exam A first (about 25 min), a 10-minute break, then Exam B (about 25 min). This also doubles as the load test, with 20 people writing at once in each block.
