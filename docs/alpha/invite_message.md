Subject: Help test a new writing app? (adults 18+, about 15 minutes, whenever suits you)

Hi [FIRST NAME],

I'm inviting a small group of adults to help test **Original**, a writing app I'm developing, before it's used with real classes. This is a calibration test of the software. It checks the app, not you. There are no grades, and no conclusions will be drawn about anyone's writing.

**Who can take part:** adults aged 18 or older who are happy to do the steps below.

**What I'd need from you, in order:**

1. **Consent form first** (5 minutes): [CONSENT FORM LINK]. It explains what's collected (including that standard web logs record IP address and browser type) and that your data is deleted within 14 days after you withdraw or I close your participation, whichever comes first. Encrypted backups age out within about 30 days after that.
2. **Four past papers you wrote yourself.** After you've joined with your personal link (**[JOIN LINK]**; it's yours alone, so please don't forward it), open your Collections home page and upload them one at a time under **Share a past paper** (a PDF, Word or text file, or pasted text). If that doesn't work for you, email them to me at arclark555@gmail.com instead. Please remove anything private (names, grades, health details) first.

That's all I need from you. There's no exam to sit and nothing new to write. The **Instructions** button at the top of the app repeats these steps.

**What happens next.** I review each paper. I keep three as your reference writing and set one aside for the test, so one paper may keep showing as *waiting for review*. That's expected. To check the software, I then write one extra piece in the style of your papers, with help from an AI writing tool, and compare that piece and your set-aside paper with the other three. The imitation is labelled as mine in the test data, is never presented as your work, and isn't shown to other volunteers. You won't see a score or any reading of your writing, and nobody else will see one as a judgement of you. I only look at grouped results.

If you don't have four separate papers, one long paper is fine: with your permission it can be split into sections (when uploading a section, tick "This is a section of a longer paper"), and those pieces are labelled as split-derived in the test data.

Afterwards I'll send a short feedback form about how the upload went.

You can stop at any time, for any reason, and ask for early deletion. Just contact me at **arclark555@gmail.com**.

Thank you,
Andrew
