Privacy policy

Applies to Bluebook, the online written-examination service, used through a free self-serve workspace.

Who we are

Bluebook is operated by [company legal name and address] (“we”). Questions about this policy: hello@originalvoice.io.

What Bluebook is for

A teacher creates a workspace, adds students by email, and sets timed written examinations. Students write their answers in the browser and submit them; the teacher reads, marks, and returns them. When a teacher uses Bluebook for a school, we process student information on the school's and teacher's behalf and only to provide the service.

What we collect

WhoWhatWhy
TeachersName, email address, a one-way hash of the password, the date you accepted these termsTo run your account and workspace
StudentsName and email address (entered by the teacher), a one-way hash of the password once set, which courses they are onTo let students sign in and see their own exams
ExamsQuestions, timing, and settings the teacher entersTo deliver the exam
SubmissionsThe answers a student writes; word count; start, deadline and submission times; whether it was late; a list of exam-mode events (for example “left full-screen” or “switched tab”) with their timesSo the teacher can read the work and see its context
MarksAny mark and written feedback the teacher recordsTo return results to the student when the teacher releases them
Security recordsAn audit log of account and data actions (for example sign-ins, exports, deletions)To keep the service secure and accountable

We do not record keystrokes, screens, cameras, microphones, or location, and Bluebook does not analyse a student's writing style or compare it with earlier work. Exam-mode events are context for the teacher, not evidence of misconduct.

Where it is stored and who processes it

  • Hosting and database: Render, in the United States [confirm region].
  • Email (invitations and password resets only): SendGrid (Twilio), when enabled.
  • Error reports (technical errors without request bodies): Sentry, when enabled.
  • Encrypted off-site backups: [object-storage provider].

We do not sell personal information, show advertising, or use student work to train models.

How long we keep it

Data is kept while the workspace uses it. A teacher can permanently delete a student and all their work at any time from the course roster or the Students page; that also removes the student's login. We do not yet delete data automatically after a fixed period. Deleted data can remain in backups for up to [30] days before those backups expire.

Your choices and rights

Students: ask your teacher to correct or delete your information, or write to us. Teachers: you can change your password, delete students and courses, and ask us to close your workspace and delete its data. Depending on where you live (for example under FERPA, state student-privacy laws, or the GDPR) you may have further rights; contact us and we will help.

Children

Bluebook is intended for students aged 13 and over. A school or teacher that uses it with younger students is responsible for obtaining any consent the law requires before adding them.

Security

Connections are encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Invitation and reset links work once and expire after 14 days. Each teacher's workspace is isolated from every other workspace.

Changes

If we change this policy materially we will say so in the app before the change takes effect.
