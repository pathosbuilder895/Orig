Privacy policy

Version 2026-10-01-draft

Draft last revised 13 October 2026. Applies to Original Collections, the online written-exam service (a digital replacement for the blue book exam), and to Original, its optional writing-consistency engine.

Who we are

Original Collections is operated by [company legal name and address] (“we”). Questions about this policy: hello@originalvoice.io.

What the service is for

A teacher creates a private workspace, adds students by email, and sets timed written exams. Students write their answers in the browser and submit them; the teacher reads, marks and returns them. Each workspace is kept separate from every other. We process student information on the teacher’s behalf (and, where there is one, the school’s), and only to provide the service.

What is kept from each exam sitting

  • The answers the student submits: the full text, one answer per question.
  • Times and length: when the sitting started and its deadline, when it was submitted, whether it arrived more than 5 minutes after the deadline, how many minutes it took, and its word count.
  • Notes for the teacher. A short note is made each time the student moved away from the exam window, the exam tab went into the background, full screen was turned off, printing or saving was tried, or a paste was tried while pasting was turned off for that exam. Only the kind of note and its time are kept, at most 500 notes per sitting: never what was on screen and never what was pasted. Notes are context for the teacher, not evidence of anything.
  • Paste counts. Blocked paste attempts are kept as notes, as above. The student’s browser also counts paste attempts on the device; that count is sent to Original only when Original is switched on for the workspace, and it is not stored.
  • A draft on the student’s own device. While a student writes, their browser keeps a copy of the answers on that device, so that a refresh or a dropped connection loses nothing. That copy is removed once the exam has been delivered. For a signed-in student, a copy of the unsubmitted answers is also kept on our server (saved about every 30 seconds and when the page is closed) so the exam can be continued from another browser. Only that student can read it; teachers cannot. It is deleted when the exam is submitted, and with the rest of the student’s data on withdrawal.

What we do not collect

  • We do not record keystrokes, typing rhythm or any other keystroke data, and we do not record screens, cameras, microphones or location.
  • An IP address is not stored in our database. (Request logs are described below.)
  • We use no cookies. Signing in is remembered in the browser’s own local storage and cleared on sign-out.
  • We use no analytics and no advertising. The exam app and these pages load no fonts, scripts or trackers from other companies.

Original, the writing-consistency engine

Original is off by default. It is switched on only for a teacher who asks for it, with their informed consent. It is in its testing phase: it has not yet been validated on real student writing. Its results are a reason for a conversation between teacher and student, never proof of anything, and they never change a grade or start a misconduct process on their own.

Where Original is on, a submitted exam is compared with earlier writing that the teacher has approved as reference writing for that student (their “writing profile”). Sealing an exam never adds it to the profile by itself; only the teacher’s approval does, and the teacher can undo it. We then also keep the reference writing the teacher approved and measurements of it, a consistency reading for each comparison with a record of it in the audit log, and a 0 to 100 reading on the submission. Teachers see these with a testing-phase label. Students never see a score in Collections.

Request logs

Our server writes a short log line for each request it handles: the time, the method and page path, the result and how long it took, the network (IP) address the request came from and the browser’s description of itself (user agent). On our host that address is usually the host’s own proxy rather than the student’s. These logs are kept by our hosting provider, not in our database, are used only to run and secure the service, and are kept for [owner: Render’s log retention for the pilot plan]. Error reports, if switched on, carry no request bodies, no IP address and no personal data.

  • Invitation and password-reset links work once and expire after 14 days.

Deleting data

A teacher can permanently delete a student from the course roster or the Students page. That removes the student’s submissions, sittings, notes, marks and login, and, where Original is on, their writing profile (if Original holds approved reference writing, the teacher removes those approvals first). What remains: one audit entry recording the deletion, sign-in records that hold the email address used, and copies in backups until those expire.

To close a workspace or delete a teacher account, write to us and we will do it by hand; there is no button for it yet.

Your choices and rights

Students can see their own submitted work and, once released, their marks and feedback under “My submissions”. To correct or delete information, ask your teacher or write to us. Teachers can change their password, delete students, and ask us to close their workspace and delete its data. Depending on where you live (for example under FERPA, state student-privacy laws or the GDPR) you may have further rights; contact us and we will help.

Children

Original Collections is intended for students aged 13 and over. A teacher or school that uses it with younger students is responsible for obtaining any consent the law requires before adding them. The service itself does not check ages. [owner and counsel: confirm the student age range for this pilot; younger students bring COPPA duties]

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), and browsers are told to use HTTPS only. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes (PBKDF2 with SHA-256, 240,000 rounds). Backups are encrypted before they are uploaded. Student sign-ins last up to 7 days (12 hours from an exam link) and teacher sign-ins 8 hours. Each teacher’s workspace is isolated from every other workspace.

Changes

If we change this policy materially we will say so in the app before the change takes effect.
