1. The service

Original Collections, a digital replacement for the blue book exam, lets a teacher run timed written exams online: create courses and exams, invite students, read and mark their answers, release results, and export them. During this pilot, workspaces are by invitation. Each workspace has limits (currently 200 students, 50 exams, and 500 submissions a month) that we may change with notice.

2. Your account

Please give correct information and keep your password private. You are responsible for activity in your workspace. Tell us promptly at hello@originalvoice.io if you think your account has been misused.

3. Adding students

By adding a student you confirm that you are allowed to do so and that the student has been told how Original Collections is used; the student notice is written for this. Do not add anyone under 13 unless the consent the law requires has been obtained. [counsel: terms for a teacher using the service without a school agreement]

4. What exam mode can and cannot do

Depending on the settings you choose for an exam, Collections opens it full screen, turns off copy and paste in the answer box, and makes a short note, with its time, when a student moves away from the exam window, the tab goes into the background, full screen is turned off, or printing, saving or pasting is tried. It cannot stop a student using another device or application, and it does not detect plagiarism. A note is context for you, not evidence: treat it as a reason to talk with the student, never, on its own, as proof of anything.

5. Original, the optional writing-consistency engine

Original is off by default and is switched on for a workspace only at the teacher’s request. It is in its testing phase and has not yet been validated on real student writing. Where it is on:

  • its results support your judgement and never replace it; it makes no grade change or misconduct decision on its own, and you should not treat a result as proof of anything;
  • a student’s sealed work joins their writing profile only if you approve it, and you can undo that approval;
  • you agree to tell your students that their sealed work may be used as reference writing, which the student notice and the exam briefing also say.

6. Acceptable use

Do not use Original Collections to break the law, to upload material you have no right to share, to harass anyone, or to probe, overload, or get around its security.

7. Your content

You and your students keep ownership of what you write. You give us permission to store and process it only to provide the service. How we handle personal information, how long we keep it, and who processes it for us are described in the privacy policy.

8. Availability and changes

We aim to keep the service available but cannot promise it will never be interrupted. A student’s answers are kept on their own device while they write and are retried if the connection drops, but please plan important exams with a fallback. We may change or discontinue features; for a material change to a paid plan we will give notice.

9. Ending

You may stop using Original Collections at any time and ask us to delete your workspace; we do that by hand on request. We may suspend a workspace that breaks these terms.
