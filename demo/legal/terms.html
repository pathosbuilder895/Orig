Terms of service

Between you and [company legal name].

1. The service

Bluebook lets a teacher run timed written examinations online: create courses and exams, invite students, read and mark their answers, and export them. The free workspace has limits (currently 200 students, 50 exams, and 500 submissions a month) that we may change with notice.

2. Your account

You must give accurate information and keep your password private. You are responsible for activity in your workspace. Tell us promptly at hello@originalvoice.io if you think your account has been misused.

3. Adding students

By adding a student you confirm that you are authorised to do so (for example by your school) and that the student has been told how Bluebook is used — the student notice is written for this. You must not add anyone under 13 unless the consent the law requires has been obtained.

4. What exam mode can and cannot do

Bluebook runs exams full-screen, disables copy and paste in the answer box, and records when a student leaves full-screen or switches tabs. It cannot stop a student using another device or application, and it is not a proctoring or plagiarism-detection service. Do not treat an exam-mode event, on its own, as proof of misconduct.

5. Acceptable use

Do not use Bluebook to break the law, to upload material you have no right to share, to harass anyone, or to probe, overload, or circumvent its security.

6. Your content

You and your students keep ownership of what you write. You give us permission to store and process it only to provide the service. Our handling of personal information is described in the privacy policy.

7. Availability and changes

We aim to keep Bluebook available but do not guarantee it will be uninterrupted. Write important exams with a fallback plan. We may change or discontinue features; for a material change to a paid plan we will give notice.

8. Ending

You may stop using Bluebook and ask us to delete your workspace at any time. We may suspend a workspace that breaks these terms.
