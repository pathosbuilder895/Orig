For students: what Bluebook records

Version 2026-10-01-draft

Your teacher uses Bluebook for some of your written exams. Here is exactly what it keeps.

When you sit an exam

  • Your answers. What you type is saved on your device while you write, and sent to your teacher when you submit.
  • Times. When you started, your deadline, when you submitted, and whether it was late.
  • Exam-mode events. If you leave full-screen, switch tabs or windows, or try to paste, the type of event and its time are recorded so your teacher has context. Accidents happen; these are not treated as proof of anything.

What Bluebook does not do

  • It does not record your keystrokes, your screen, your camera, your microphone, or your location.
  • It does not analyse your writing style or compare it with your earlier work.
  • Your answers are not used to train any model and are never sold.

Who sees your work

Only your teacher (and people your school authorises). Marks and feedback appear in “My submissions” once your teacher releases them.

Questions or deletion

Ask your teacher, or write to hello@originalvoice.io. Your teacher can delete your account and all your work. More detail is in the privacy policy.
