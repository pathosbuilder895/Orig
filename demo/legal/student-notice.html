For students: what Original Collections keeps

Version 2026-10-01-draft

Draft last revised 13 October 2026. Your teacher uses Original Collections for some of your written exams. Here is exactly what it keeps, and what it does not.

When you sit an exam

  • Your answers. While you write, a copy is kept in your browser on your own device, so a refresh or a dropped connection does not lose your work. A copy of your unsubmitted answers is also kept on our server, so you can carry on from another browser if you need to. Only you can see that copy; your teacher cannot. When you submit, your answers go to your teacher, and both copies are removed.
  • Times and length. When you started, your deadline, when you submitted, whether it was more than 5 minutes after the deadline, how long it took, and your word count.
  • Short notes for your teacher. If you moved away from the exam window, the exam tab went into the background, full screen was turned off, printing or saving was tried, or a paste was tried while pasting was turned off, a note is made. Only the kind of note and its time are kept: never what was on your screen and never what you tried to paste. You see these notes too, as they happen. They are context for your teacher, not an accusation, and short moments away are normal.
  • Paste attempts. Blocked pastes become notes, as above. If your teacher uses Original (below), the number of paste attempts is also sent with your work for comparison, and it is not stored.
  • Your phone, if your teacher uses phone parking. The phone page sends only the name or initials you type and whether the page is open. This is cleared out once it is 24 hours old, the next time phone parking is used.

What is never kept

  • No keystrokes or typing rhythm, and nothing from your screen, camera, microphone or location.
  • Your IP address is not stored in our database.
  • Your answers are not used to train any model, and they are never sold.

Original, if your teacher uses it

Original is a tool that compares a new piece of your writing with your own earlier writing. It is off by default. If your teacher uses it, the exam briefing tells you before you begin.

  • It is in its testing phase: it has not yet been checked on real student writing.
  • A result is a starting point for a conversation with your teacher, never proof of anything. It never changes your grade on its own.
  • Your teacher may choose to add a sealed exam of yours to your writing profile (the earlier writing new work is compared with), and can undo that. Nothing is added automatically.
  • Collections never shows you a score.

Who sees your work

Your teacher, and anyone your school authorises. Your marks and feedback appear in “My submissions” once your teacher releases them.

Questions or deletion

Ask your teacher, or write to hello@originalvoice.io. Your teacher can delete your account and all your work. More detail, including how long things are kept, is in the privacy policy.
