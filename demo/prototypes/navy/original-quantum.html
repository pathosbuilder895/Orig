How the number is made

Each of the 97 measured habits is compared with the range this student shows across their teacher-approved samples. The deviation summarises how far the paper sits from those ranges, taken together: tanh(rms z / k).

The density matrix ρ = Σᵢ wᵢ |φᵢ⟩⟨φᵢ| summarises the same approved samples, and the fingerprint above is an illustration of it. It does not produce the deviation number.

With few approved samples the ranges are wide and uncertain, so an honest paper on a new topic can still drift. Read the side-by-side evidence before the number.
