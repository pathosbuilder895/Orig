What already stands out
“

You follow a long, winding sentence with a very short one. It happens in every piece you've written: it's the single most recognisable thing about your prose.

“

You almost never hedge. Where most writers reach for perhaps or it seems, you state the position and then defend it.

“

You open paragraphs with the argument itself, not with a transition word. Only 1 in 9 of your paragraphs begins with however or furthermore.

“

You use the em dash often, usually to interrupt yourself mid-thought.

What Original keeps
Your portrait is a set of numbers about how you write, nothing more.
Kept
  • Measurements of the 34 habits above, and how much they vary across your pieces.
  • The pieces your teacher approved as reference writing, so a measurement can always be traced back to its source.
  • A few Bluebook session facts: time spent, word count, paste attempts and times the window lost focus. They are context, not evidence.
Never kept
  • Anything from your screen, camera, microphone, or other applications.
  • How you type: no key-by-key timing is ever recorded.
  • Your drafts from outside a session, or what you searched while writing.
  • Any judgement of quality: Original measures likeness to you, never how good the writing is.
