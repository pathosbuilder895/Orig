Original

A teacher's view and a student's view, side by side.

Bluebook is a quiet place for students to write. Original, when a teacher switches it on, compares a new paper with the writing that teacher has approved as the student's own. It is a reason for a conversation, never a verdict. Everyone and everything on these pages is made up.
