Why Bluebook matters

Bluebook gives students a quiet, trustworthy place to write. If Original is switched on, the sealed booklets you approve become the reference writing it compares later papers against. More approved samples give it more to go on; with only a few, it may say it can't tell.

Testing phase: Original is not yet validated on real student writing. Treat any result as a reason for a conversation, never as evidence.
