Conversation Packet · Confidential

This is a starting point, not a verdict. Original measures how a paper compares with a student’s own earlier writing. A difference here is a reason to talk with the student; on its own it is never evidence.

Having the conversation a suggested approach
Worth doing
  • Open with the work, not the numbers: ask the student to walk you through the paper’s argument in their own words.
  • Show one of the habits above and ask what they notice about it.
  • Ask about their process: drafts, tools, timeline, where they wrote it.
  • Offer a way to add reference writing: a supervised piece in Bluebook, which you can approve as part of their baseline if it helps.
Worth avoiding
  • Don’t present the numbers as proof: they describe distance from the student’s own habits, nothing more.
  • Don’t open with an accusation; it forecloses the conversation the numbers are meant to open.
  • Don’t rely on memory: write down what was said and what you agreed, on the outcome line below.
