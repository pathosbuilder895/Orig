Why Bluebook matters

Bluebook gives students a quiet, trustworthy place to write. A sealed booklet is supervised writing, kept exactly as the student wrote it. It joins that student's baseline only when you approve it, and you can undo that choice. Original compares later papers with a student's baseline only when Original is on for your workspace, and with only a few samples it may say it can't tell.
