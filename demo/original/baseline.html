Papers students shared
Students can share their own past papers from their Collections account. A shared paper joins the baseline only when you approve it, and it counts at the lower weight of a student upload, not as supervised writing. Nothing is read or compared until you approve. A paper marked as a section of a longer paper is tagged split-derived for calibration. Read this paper shows its text without adding it. Undo approval takes an approved paper back out of the baseline, and it waits for your approval again; nothing else changes.
