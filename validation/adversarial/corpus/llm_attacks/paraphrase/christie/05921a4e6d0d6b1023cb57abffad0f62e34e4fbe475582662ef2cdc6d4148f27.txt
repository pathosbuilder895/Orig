I’m ashamed to own it, I guess, but I came over here bent on finding her and fixing everything up, and taking her home as Mrs. Julius P. Hersheimmer!” “Oh!” said Tommy, astounded. Julius abruptly uncrossed his legs and went on: “Just goes to show what an almighty fool a man can make of himself! Julius glanced at him and thought it wise to drop the subject. They told the driver to go slowly, and kept a sharp lookout so as not to miss the path. They reached it soon after leaving the town, and Tommy promptly stopped the car, asked offhandedly whether the path went down to the sea, and on hearing that it did paid the man off generously. A moment later the taxi was chugging slowly back toward Holyhead.
