A half-groan escaped Tommy's lips. If only Tuppence could have been at his side to witness the triumphant conclusion of their joint venture! Then he resolutely pushed the thought of Tuppence aside. His confidence in Sir James was growing. There was a man who would unfailingly find out where Tuppence was. Until then, though, Jane Finn! And suddenly a fear gripped his heart. It seemed too easy.... Suppose they found her dead ... struck down by the hand of Mr. Brown? A minute later he was already laughing at these melodramatic fancies. The doctor held open the door of a room, and they went in. On the white bed, bandages around her head, lay the girl. Somehow the whole scene seemed unreal. Ten minutes later the two young men were sitting in a first-class compartment _en route_ for Chester. For a long time neither of them spoke. When Julius finally broke the silence, it was with a completely unexpected remark. "Say," he observed thoughtfully, "have you ever made a darned fool of yourself over a girl's face?" They told the driver to go slowly and kept a sharp lookout so as not to miss the path.
